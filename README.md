# Travel-Tech & Hospitality-Tech Portfolio
AI Product Development | Technical Product Management | Business Transformation

About This Portfolio

This repository documents my practical exploration and development of technology-enabled products for the travel and hospitality industry.

Combining professional experience in enterprise business analysis, SaaS product management and business transformation with an MBA specialisation in Tourism, Travel and Hospitality Management, I am developing practical solutions addressing emerging challenges across travel distribution, hospitality operations, customer personalisation and AI-driven business processes.

The objective is to bridge business strategy, product management and technical implementation.

Portfolio Projects

02. Hospitality Operations AI Agent

An AI-enabled operational coordination platform designed to streamline guest requests, departmental workflows, task allocation and operational escalation within hotels.

A simulated hotel distribution ecosystem demonstrating interactions between a Property Management System, channel manager and online travel distribution channels.

A hotel commercial intelligence solution analysing occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, booking trends and distribution performance.
